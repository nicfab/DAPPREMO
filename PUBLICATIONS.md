# Publications

This file records the publication history of DAPPREMO and the rights regime of
each source. It exists for a practical reason: the specification in this
repository is an original text, and whether a given publication may feed that
text — or may only be cited — depends on the terms under which it was
published. Those terms differ from one work to another.

The specification refers to this file rather than restating the information, so
that a new publication is recorded by updating one row.

## Translations

An Italian translation of the JSCI 2021 article was published in 2023 (see the
record above). It is a translation of a *publication*, not of this
specification, and its rights regime is that of its own publisher. A translation
of the specification into any language is therefore an original translation of
the text in this repository, made under CC BY 4.0, and does not derive from the
published translations.

## How this record is used

Two distinct things are recorded above, and they should not be conflated.

**Authority.** The most recent peer-reviewed version of the model is the 2024
book chapter. Where the specification and an earlier publication diverge on a
point of substance, the later publication prevails.

**Reusability.** Only the JSCI 2021 version carries a rights regime permitting
its text to be reworked into the specification. This is a constraint on
*wording*, not on *substance*: ideas, concepts and mathematical relations are
not protected by copyright, so the specification may state what the later
publications establish, provided it does so in its own words and cites them.

## Adding a publication

When a new work on DAPPREMO is published, add a row recording its rights
regime alongside its bibliographic details, and — if it supersedes the current
most recent peer-reviewed version — update the `preferred-citation` entry in
[`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff) and the references section of the
specification. A new publication does not by itself require a new version of
the specification: it does so only where it changes the substance of the model.

For works deposited by the author on preprint servers, the licence is selected
at deposit. Selecting CC BY 4.0 makes the deposited text directly reusable
here, and aligns the preprint with the licence applied to this repository.
