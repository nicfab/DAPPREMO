# Publications

This file records the publication history of DAPPREMO and the rights regime of
each source. It exists for a practical reason: the specification in this
repository is an original text, and whether a given publication may feed that
text — or may only be cited — depends on the terms under which it was
published. Those terms differ from one work to another.

The specification refers to this file rather than restating the information, so
that a new publication is recorded by updating one row.

### Note on the 2023 article

Two things are true of this article at once, and both bear on reuse.

The author retains all rights in it: it was submitted directly to the journal,
no assignment was executed, and the publication carries no copyright notice or
licence statement. Nothing in the publisher's terms restricts what the author
may do with the text.

The article also declares itself an updated version of the corresponding
chapter of the goWare 2020 book, whose rights are reserved to that publisher.
Reusing substantial passages of the Italian text therefore requires checking
them against the goWare publishing agreement as well, not only against the
position of IJ Editores. Passages that restate the model in wording original to
the 2023 article are not affected; passages carried over from the 2020 chapter
are.

Note also that "freely readable" is not the same as open access in the technical
sense: no open licence has been applied, so third parties may not reuse the text.
The permission recorded here runs to the author alone.

## Translations

An Italian translation of the JSCI 2021 article was published in 2023 (see the
record above). It is a translation of a *publication*, not of this
specification.

The Italian text of this repository was written as an original translation of
the English specification, and remains so: it does not derive from the 2023
publication. That the 2023 text is now known to be reusable by the author does
not change what has already been written, and any future translation of the
specification into any language is likewise an original translation of the text
in this repository, made under CC BY 4.0.

## How this record is used

Two distinct things are recorded above, and they should not be conflated.

**Authority.** The most recent peer-reviewed version of the model is the 2024
book chapter. Where the specification and an earlier publication diverge on a
point of substance, the later publication prevails.

**Reusability.** Two publications carry a rights regime permitting their text to
be reworked into the specification: the JSCI 2021 article in English, and its
2023 Italian translation — the latter subject to the note above concerning its
relationship to the 2020 book chapter. The remaining publications may be cited
only. This is a constraint on *wording*, not on *substance*: ideas, concepts and
mathematical relations are not protected by copyright, so the specification may
state what the later publications establish, provided it does so in its own
words and cites them.

## Adding a publication

When a new work on DAPPREMO is published, add a row recording its rights
regime alongside its bibliographic details, and — if it supersedes the current
most recent peer-reviewed version — update the `preferred-citation` entry in
[`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff) and the references section of the
specification. A new publication does not by itself require a new version of
the specification: it does so only where it changes the substance of the model.

Record the rights regime as ascertained, not as presumed. A commercial
publisher does not necessarily hold the rights: where an author submits a work
directly and executes no assignment, the rights remain with the author even
though the publication carries no statement to that effect. Where the position
cannot be established, record it as unverified rather than inferring it.

For works deposited by the author on preprint servers, the licence is selected
at deposit. Selecting CC BY 4.0 makes the deposited text directly reusable
here, and aligns the preprint with the licence applied to this repository.
